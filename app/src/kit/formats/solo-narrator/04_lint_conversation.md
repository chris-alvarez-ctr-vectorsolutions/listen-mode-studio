## D. Uncanny-valley lint

| Check | Starting target |
| --- | --- |
| Pivot lines before a new section | One per section, on their own line |
| Questions to the listener per topic part | At least one, each answered |
| Average sentence length | Under about 15 words, with a mix of short and long |
| Sentences over 20 words | None |
| Fragments per part | Four at most, for rhythm and emphasis |
| Concrete, visual details per part | At least one |
| Light or funny moments per part | One at most |
| Each way in and way out | Used once per listen |
| Delivery tags per part | Four to eight, mostly on pivots and questions |
| CAPS emphasis per part | One or two words |
| Em dashes | None |

## E. Narration

- [ ] The open has a teaser and one promise line, with no background about the narrator, and stays under 60 words.
- [ ] Every part states its one big idea near the start and lands on it near the end.
- [ ] Every turn is signposted, and each new section starts with a one-line pivot.
- [ ] Every topic part asks the listener at least one question and answers it.
- [ ] The narrator talks to "you" and never tells the listener how they feel.
- [ ] There is only one speaker, and no part refers to another part, episode or listen.
- [ ] Every setup has its payoff in the same part. No "hold onto that picture."
- [ ] Nothing ("the rule," "that step") is referred to before it's been introduced.
- [ ] No example wording from the production guide, lint checklist or pattern library appears in the script.
- [ ] No line repeats a line from the voice example, and no part follows its sequence of moves unless its beat sheet chose them.
- [ ] No two parts in a listen share the same overall shape.
- [ ] Read aloud, every line sounds like something a person would say out loud.
