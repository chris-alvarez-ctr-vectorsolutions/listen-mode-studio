## 10. Editing in ElevenLabs Studio

Delivery gets fixed in Studio. Content gets fixed in the script.

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Mispronounced word or glitch | Regenerate the words in Studio; add to the pronunciation dictionary |
| Flat or wrong emotion | Change the tag or punctuation in Studio, regenerate, copy the change back to the script |
| Pause or SFX timing | Studio timeline, or leave it to the assembly engine |
| The words themselves are wrong | Back to the drafting stage, so the new words pass the validity pass |
