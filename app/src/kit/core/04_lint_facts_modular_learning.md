# Validity pass and lint checklist

Stage 6 runs every check below and reports pass or fail, with the line and a fix for each failure. Starting targets are marked; tune them by ear after a few modules.

## A. Facts (validity)

- [ ] Every claim-bearing line maps to a ledger ID in the trace table.
- [ ] No claim is added to, strengthened, generalized or combined.
- [ ] Every example list appears in the source content or the ledger.
- [ ] No statistic, date, count or source appears that the ledger doesn't hold.
- [ ] Flagged claims are marked and used only as the ledger allows.
- [ ] No host or character moment carries a fact, injury, exposure or outcome.
- [ ] Flag, under "For the SME," any added detail the ledger doesn't state: how a device or process works or looks, a superlative or absolute ("most effective," "always," "the only"), what a person knew or was trained on, or a consequence. Quote the line. Don't cut it silently, and don't resolve it in the script.
- [ ] No real company is named as an employer.
- **Any new fact:** list it for the SME with the line it came from. Don't cut it silently.

## B. Modular rules

- [ ] The open only promises content from positions that play in every listen.
- [ ] No part refers to another part ("again," "earlier," "like we said").
- [ ] Every Test-up and Reinforced version stands alone, and none assumes the Base was heard.
- [ ] Every part ID is in the pathway table, and every table part is present. No remedial or review content appears.
- [ ] There are no bridges, and each part's first exchange works as a signpost: it brings the topic up conversationally (a different move in each part, never the same opening formula twice, and a co-speaker's name used to open at most one part), names it plainly, never uses a label like "New topic:", and says what kind of thing comes next, before any story or content begins.
- [ ] Every voice other than the host is introduced by name and role, with a hand-off, right before its first line. No voice starts speaking cold after being mentioned in the third person.

## C. Learning

- [ ] Each part serves one objective and carries its required claims.
- [ ] Each Know part restates the core rule once, near the end.
- [ ] Feel parts don't instruct. Perceive parts don't tell the listener what to do.
- [ ] Any judgment hold asks a question that tests that part's objective.
- [ ] Any judgment hold is a question spoken directly to the listener ("let's ask our audience..."), followed by the 3-second hold, then a hand-back line that invites the answerer to respond ("Okay, [name], let's hear your answer now."). No hold follows a question one speaker asks another.
- [ ] Every learning outcome in the analysis (if provided) maps to at least one part.

## D. Conversation feel

- [ ] The host adds reactions, curiosity or restatements, not only questions.
- [ ] The host never states or believes anything incorrect, and any mistaken belief is attributed to "people" and corrected in full right away.
- [ ] No "Okay," "Got it," "Right" or "Huh" opens turn after turn. Each is used at most once per part.
- [ ] No stacked questions.
- [ ] Parts don't all share one pattern, and no Feel part or story ends on a stated lesson from the host or expert.
- [ ] No "uhh." Fillers are rare, different each time, and CAPS stay at one or two per part.
